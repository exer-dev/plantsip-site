Koliko dugo čuvamo podatke

Dok ne obrišeš nalog. Nalog brišeš u aplikaciji: Podešavanja → Obriši nalog. Tada se odmah brišu sve biljke, fotografije, istorija, lokacija i ostali podaci. Kopije u rezervnim kopijama servisa brišu se u njihovom redovnom ciklusu. Premium pretplatu otkazuješ u Google Play-u.

Tvoja prava

Imaš pravo na pristup, ispravku, brisanje, ograničenje obrade, prenosivost podataka i prigovor, kao i pravo da podneseš pritužbu Povereniku za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti (www.poverenik.rs) ili nadležnom nadzornom organu u EU. Za sve zahteve piši na support@plantsip.app.

English

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Plantsip is a plant-watering reminder app. The data controller is Emin Bajramović, Novi Sad, Serbia (contact: support@plantsip.app). We process personal data in accordance with the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we process and why

Data Purpose Legal basis
Email address Sign-in with an emailed code Contract
Plants: nickname, species, room, pot, soil, light, photos Watering schedules and reminders Contract
Watering history and your feedback ("still moist", "was dry") Adapting the schedule Contract
Town (name and town coordinates, not your address), heating/AC Weather: rain, frost, heat Contract
Time zone, language, reminder time Reminders at the right time, in your language Contract
Push notification token Reminders and weather alerts Contract
Subscription status (Premium) Unlocking Premium features Contract
Number of plant recognitions used Free plan limits Legitimate interest

We show no ads, sell no data and build no advertising profiles.

Who receives data (processors)

Service What it receives Where
Supabase (database, sign-in, photos, server functions) Everything above EU (Frankfurt)
Plant.id / Kindwise The photo of the plant you scan EU
Anthropic (Claude) Only the plant's scientific name, for a general care profile — no personal data USA
Open-Meteo Town coordinates, no data about you —
Expo and Google Firebase (push) Device token and notification text (e.g. plant nicknames) USA
RevenueCat and Google Play Purchase data and your in-app ID USA
Resend Your email address, to send the sign-in code USA

Transfers to the USA rely on GDPR mechanisms (standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, depending on the service).

Retention

Until you delete your account: Settings → Delete account removes all plants, photos, history, location and other data immediately. Copies in the providers' backups are removed in their regular cycle. Cancel a Premium subscription in Google Play.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing, and to complain to the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (www.poverenik.rs) or your EU supervisory authority. Contact us at support@plantsip.app.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.
